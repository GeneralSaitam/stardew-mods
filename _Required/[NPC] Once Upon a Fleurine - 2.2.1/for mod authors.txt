Hello! I'm writing this as a way to make compat easier for mod authors. I'm going to add a list of Fleurine's conversation topics, but just keep in mind that she has.. a lot! I added some in dialogue lines for herself and townies, so there's a bunch that might just be throwaway, and there is no need to use them all!

ADDED BY EVENTS / IMPORTANT.
    - FleurineIntro: Used to share an NPCs thoughts/opinions on Fleurine.
    - FleurineTouchofClass: Fleurine goes on a little rampage about wanting statues and a gazebo in the town square. Used to share your NPC's thoughts.
    - FleurineFriendlyRivalry: Fleurine and Elliott debate about which is better: poetry or fashion. This CT is used to share your NPC's opinion.,
    - FleurineGoodDeeds: Fleurine bakes a pie for everyone in town, including your NPC. Used to share your NPC's thoughts about this.
    - FleurineFairytale: Can be used by friends/family of Jas and/or Vincent. Fleurine tells Jas and Vincent a story about a princess.
    - FleurRasSpellFail: Used by anyone who frequents the Cindersap Forest. Fleurine and the Wizard had a spell failure/explosion and rush out of the tower.
    - EllineDuet: Used by Elliott's friends. Fleurine and Elliott perform a duet together. They kiss for the first time. Triggers only if her rival hearts with Elliott is enabled.

ADDED IN CONVERSATION / MINOR.
    - CashierFleurine: Can be used by anyone who frequents Pierre's. References a time Fleurine once tried to help out at Pierre's and was 'fired' for being 'too distracting.'
    - FleurinesPoem: Can be used by friends of Elliott. References a time Fleurine asked Elliott to write her a poem and he had an 'existential crisis' over it.
    - AlexFailsFleurine: Can be used by friends of Alex. References Alex trying to impress Fleurine and failing.
    - LewisSaysNo: Can be used by anyone who frequents town square. References Fleurine requesting that she be allowed to enchant the town square and being told no.
    - FleurineRejectsAlex: Can be used by friends of Alex. References Fleurine rejecting Alex.
    - AlexInvitesFleurine: Can be used by friends of Alex. References Alex inviting Fleurine to watch him work out and being rejected.
    - FleurineEvelynBraid: Can be used by friends of Evelyn. References Fleurine braiding Evelyn's hair.
